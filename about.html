Who We Are

Kenya's Security & Technology Specialists

Gemani Supplies is one of Kenya's foremost suppliers and installers of security systems and technology infrastructure. Established over a decade ago, we have grown to serve residential, commercial, industrial and institutional clients across all 47 counties.

Our product portfolio spans CCTV surveillance, access control, electric fencing, automatic gates, solar energy, networking, telephony and computing — making us a genuine one-stop shop for all security and technology needs.

We are committed to supplying only genuine, warranty-backed products from leading global brands. Our in-house team of certified technicians ensures every installation meets the highest professional standards.
